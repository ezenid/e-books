About E - Books

E - Books is an online Ethiopian bookstore built to make classic and contemporary Ethiopian literature accessible to readers everywhere. Whether you are searching for timeless Amharic novels, modern psychological dramas, or new independent releases, our catalog brings Ethiopia's rich literary heritage straight to your screen.

We bridge the gap between traditional bookstores and modern convenience by allowing you to browse our curated collection online and choose the fulfillment option that fits your schedule—either fast doorstep delivery across Addis Ababa or direct in-shop pickup.

Our Mission & Story

Finding specific Ethiopian titles often means visiting multiple shops across the city or dealing with limited stock. E - Books was created to solve that problem with a simple, reliable online storefront where readers can easily discover great books, place orders in seconds, and support local authors.

Beyond serving readers, we also provide an open submission platform for Ethiopian writers, independent authors, and publishers to list their works and reach a wider audience.

How Ordering Works

1. Home Delivery (Addis Ababa)

Browse our catalog, select your desired books by ID on our order page, and enter your delivery address. Our team confirms your order and delivers your books directly to your home or office.

2. Shop Pickup

Prefer to pick up your order in person? Select the shop pickup option when placing your order, and we will prepare your books so they are ready for quick collection at our Addis Ababa location during store hours.

What We Offer

  • Curated Ethiopian Literature: A growing collection of celebrated classics, historical fiction, and contemporary bestsellers.
  • Flexible Fulfillment: Convenient home delivery across Addis Ababa or fast in-shop pickup.
  • Simple Payment Options: Pay easily via Cash on Delivery/Pickup, Telebirr, or CBE Birr / Bank Transfer.
  • Author & Publisher Submissions: A dedicated channel for authors to submit their books to our store catalog.
